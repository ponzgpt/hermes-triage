Method and limits

  • Read-only: one GraphQL query per 100 open issues. Nothing is written to GitHub.
  • A response is the first comment by anyone other than the author that is not a bot; a maintainer response must come from someone who merged one of the last 300 merged pull requests (OutThisLife, alt-glitch, austinpickett, benbarclay, ethernet8023, kshitijk4poor, teknium1, unsupportedpastels, yoniebans), because GitHub hides org membership from outsiders. Only the first 10 comments are read, and times measure open issues only, so they say nothing about issues already closed.
  • Labels and priorities come from transparent regular expressions over the title and the first 1,500 characters of the body, plus the bug form's Affected Component field. Existing maintainer labels always win. P1 is only suggested on strong signals (data loss, security, cannot start).
  • Duplicates are TF-IDF cosine matches over titles and code identifiers (threshold 0.55): candidates for a person to confirm, never to close automatically.
